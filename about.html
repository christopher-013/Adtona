Adtona: a free AI travel planner and itinerary builder

Adtona turns a destination and a set of dates into three things you can actually use: a shareable trip website, a printable day-by-day itinerary, and an AI-ready planning file you can hand to ChatGPT or Claude. It runs entirely in your browser, and it is free — no account, no API key, no usage charge.

How planning a trip with Adtona works

  1. Enter a destination and dates. Type any city and Adtona confirms it against a geocoder before researching it.
  2. Swipe through suggestions. Places to see, eat, and shop are drawn from Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, and OpenStreetMap, ranked so the landmarks a first-time visitor expects come first. Keep what appeals, skip what does not.
  3. Answer a few questions. Pace, home base, must-dos, bookings, and anything to avoid — each one optional.
  4. Get your guide. Adtona builds the itinerary around opening hours, meal windows, and travel time, then exports the whole trip as a website, a printable plan, and a structured file.

What makes Adtona different

  • Free, with no sign-up. There is nothing to create an account for and no key to paste.
  • Your trip stays on your device. The draft lives in your browser's own storage, not on a server.
  • Works with the AI you already use. Export the plan, refine it in ChatGPT or Claude, and import the result back — one source of truth rather than a chat log.
  • A guide you can share. The exported website works on a phone, can be hosted anywhere, and keeps working offline once it has been opened.
  • Real sources. Suggestions and photos come from Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap, Wikimedia Commons, and Open-Meteo — not invented.

The name

Adtona comes from the Bisaya (Cebuano) phrase “Adto na” — “Go now.”

Questions travellers ask

Is Adtona free?
Yes. Adtona is currently free to use in a modern web browser. There is no account to create, API key to provide, or usage charge.
What does Adtona create for my trip?
Adtona creates a shareable trip website, printable itinerary, and AI-ready planning file based on your destination, preferences, bookings, and constraints.
Does Adtona book travel?
No. Adtona helps organize and plan a trip, but travelers should complete and verify bookings directly with airlines, hotels, restaurants, attractions, and other providers.
Can I use Adtona with ChatGPT or Claude?
Yes. Adtona can export a structured planning file and prompts that can be used with ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI assistant. An updated plan can then be imported back into Adtona.
Does Adtona store my trip data?
Your saved trip draft stays in your browser. Destination, weather, map, feedback, and related features may contact the external services described in the Privacy information.
Can I use the guide offline?
An exported and hosted guide can support offline use after it has been opened over HTTPS and cached successfully. Offline availability depends on browser and hosting support.

Read the Adtona privacy notice for exactly what stays on your device and what is sent to Open-Meteo, Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, OpenStreetMap, and Wikimedia Commons. Adtona is in public beta. Always verify hours, prices, tickets, and entry requirements before you travel.
