Your privacy

Privacy

Your trip stays in your browser. Adtona has no account system and no trip backend, and it does not upload your plan.

What stays on this device

Your destination, dates, answers, chosen recommendations, and saved draft are stored locally in this browser using local storage. Photos you add are stored locally in IndexedDB where the browser supports it, and photo details may also be kept alongside the trip draft.

Browser storage is not encrypted by Adtona, so protect your browser profile and clear the saved draft on a shared or public computer.

Destination and weather research

To suggest real places, Adtona sends the destination you type — never your whole trip — to Open-Meteo for weather, and to Wikipedia, Wikivoyage, and OpenStreetMap/Overpass for places to see, eat, and shop. Photos for those suggestions come from Wikimedia Commons.

These providers also receive normal connection information such as your IP address, and their own privacy policies apply.

Maps

Map links and embedded maps load from Google Maps, which receives the place or map details being opened along with normal connection information. Adtona opens a map only when you choose to view one.

Feedback

Sending feedback submits the category, your summary and message, the current page, app version, browser details, and screen size to Adtona’s feedback service. It creates a public issue, so do not include private trip details, addresses, email addresses, passwords, or links. Your trip data and photos are never attached.

Exports and sharing

An export contains the trip information you chose to include, and a complete structured export can also carry locally stored photo data. Once you share or host an exported guide, anything inside it is visible to whoever can open it.

Usage counting

Adtona sends one word at three moments — when you open the app, when a guide is generated, and when you export one — so the site can tell whether it is being used at all. Nothing else goes with any of them: no destination, no dates, no answers, no identifier, and no cookie.

Each is recorded as the word, the UTC time it arrived, and the two-letter country Cloudflare resolved the request to — published in a public issue on the Adtona repository and kept for about thirteen months. The country comes from your network address, which is used to derive it and then discarded; the address itself is never stored, and when the country cannot be determined nothing is recorded in its place.

Because the entries are timed, ones close together read as a single visit — an app opening at 09:14 followed by a guide at 09:15 is plainly the same session — so the log shows the shape of a visit as well as the count of them.

It still holds nothing that identifies you: no address, cookie, account, browser or device is stored, a country is shared by everyone using the site from it, nothing distinguishes one visitor from another, and nothing connects a visit on one day to a visit on any other. Anything else reported — weekly and monthly figures, how many days had activity, averages per active day — is arithmetic on those entries rather than extra information about you. Your address is used only to rate-limit the request and to derive the country above, and is never stored.

Analytics

When the hosted site has it enabled, Cloudflare may process basic analytics and security data for adtona.com. Adtona does not run advertising or cross-site tracking.

Your controls

Starting a new trip or clearing this site’s browser storage removes the saved draft and any locally stored photos from that browser. Files on your device are never changed, moved, or deleted.
